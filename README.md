# Global Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze global supply chain performance.
The dashboard provides insights into **sales trends, product performance, customer distribution, and logistics efficiency**.

The objective of this project is to demonstrate **business intelligence and data visualization skills using Power BI**.

---

## Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* Power Query
* DAX
* Data Visualization

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of overall business performance including total revenue, profit, orders, and global sales distribution.

### Product Intelligence

Analyzes top-performing products, category profitability, and product-level revenue insights.

### Customer & Market Insights

Explores customer segments, regional market performance, and top revenue-generating countries.

### Logistics & Delivery Analysis

Evaluates shipping performance, delivery delays, and logistics efficiency across shipping modes.

### Product Deep Dive (Drillthrough)

Allows users to drill down into individual product performance including revenue trends and market distribution.

## Key Insights

* Europe generates the highest revenue among all markets.
* Consumer segment contributes more than 50% of total sales.
* Standard shipping mode handles the majority of orders.
* Same-day shipping provides the fastest delivery time.
